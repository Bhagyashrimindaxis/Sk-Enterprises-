About Us

S. K. Enterprises is a well-established modular and interior solutions company, serving customers since 2005 under the leadership of Mr. Dnyaneshwar Kakade. How We Work

Our 4-Step Furniture Process

Consultation & Planning

We visit your site or review floor plans to understand your aesthetic preference, functional storage needs, and lifestyle habits.

3D Design & Selection

Our design studio generates realistic 3D CAD renders and presents tactile samples of natural veneers, laminates, fabrics, and hardware.

Factory Manufacturing

Your order is precision-cut and edge-banded on German machinery in our Pune facility with rigorous 5-stage quality inspections.

Delivery & Installation

Protective transit packaging and white-glove on-site assembly by factory technicians, followed by thorough vacuum cleaning and handover.
